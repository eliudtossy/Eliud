CASE STUDY 01

Conveyor Drive Motor Failure

Problem

During my time at Kioo Limited, one of the motors responsible for rotating a belt conveyor suddenly stopped operating, causing the conveyor to stop.

Inspection & Diagnosis

I first performed a visual inspection of the motor and surrounding components, but no visible external abnormality was identified.

I then isolated the equipment by switching off the power supply before carrying out further inspection. After safely isolating the equipment, I disassembled the motor and inspected its internal components.

During the inspection, I found that the round drive belt inside the motor had become blocked, preventing the motor from operating normally.

Corrective Action

  • Removed the blocked belt.
  • Replaced it with a new belt.
  • Cleaned the motor and its internal components.
  • Applied appropriate lubrication.
  • Reassembled the motor correctly.

Testing & Result

After completing the repair, I restored the equipment and tested the motor. The motor started operating normally again, allowing the belt conveyor to resume operation.
